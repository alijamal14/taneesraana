FAQ

Common questions

What are the Islamabad clinic hours?

Monday to Thursday 6 pm – 8 pm, and Friday to Sunday 8 am – 8 pm. Please call ahead around public holidays.

Do you offer fertility treatment?

Yes — infertility treatment is a main focus, using modern methods: fertility evaluation for couples, ovulation induction and IUI, with IVF guidance and referral when it is the right option. You can ask privately on WhatsApp 0331‑5907960.

Where is the Nowshera clinic?

Inside MK Hospital on GT Road, Nowshera Cantonment, with free parking. Open Monday to Thursday, 10 am – 12 pm.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through the appointment calendar, message on WhatsApp 0331‑5907960, or call 0311‑5027974 (clinic landline 051‑2305845). For urgent or high‑risk concerns, please call.

Is labour pain relief available?

Yes. Pain‑relief options are discussed during pregnancy as part of an individual birth plan.
